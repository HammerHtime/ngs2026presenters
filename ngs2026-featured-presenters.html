A film made by the community, not about it.

LAPD officers, former gang members, students and families who lost loved ones picked up cameras and documented their own daily lives. Unlikely collaborators, telling one story.

The result asks one question: what happens when the people living the story tell it themselves, and start to see their enemies differently?

For investigators, prevention workers, educators and community partners, it is a case study in what a neighbourhood can do when it decides the killing stops.
